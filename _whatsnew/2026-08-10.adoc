=== Active scans tab to enhance scan management

Data Classification now provides an *Active scans* tab in the Configuration menu, providing an enhanced experience for reviewing the status of scans on individual volumes and modifying the scan settings. The Active scans tab provides an overview of all volumes activate for mapping and full scans, allowing you to individual or match modify them by prioritizing a scan, pausing a scan, or reviewing errors. 

For more information, see link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-data-classification/task-managing-repo-scanning.html[Manage scans^].
